Dear Ann…

Hearken, gentle Ann. By this missive thou art most graciously summoned to a gathering of good company, upon the fifteenth day of November, in the year of our Lord two thousand twenty-six, being the Sabbath.

The revelry shall begin at the tenth hour of the morning and endure until , which is to say hours of merriment.

Thou art bidden to bring three things: a merry spirit, a smile of good cheer, and thy younger sister. The last is no request, but a decree, and no refusal shall be heard.

Thy place is kept and thy coming is awaited. Given under seal, with great anticipation.
